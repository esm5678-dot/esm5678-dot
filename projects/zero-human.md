# PROJECT ZERO HUMAN / ZERO OPS

**Status:** Public experiment and offline prototype. [Repository](https://github.com/esm5678-dot/Project-zero-human-)

## Problem and approach
The experiment asks whether an AI-led team can build a business without additional cash spending. ZERO OPS is a small-business inquiry management hypothesis. The founder retains responsibility for consequential decisions.

## Implemented prototype
The public repository documents a browser-based offline demo using fictional inquiries, deterministic keyword classification, suggested reply drafts, status changes and CSV export. It does not call an AI model, send customer messages or persist data across refreshes. The repository's acceptance record reports eight browser checks; those checks were not rerun for this portfolio.

## Limits and next work
The prototype is not a production CRM. The repository README's customer-validation status may lag subsequent outreach; do not infer current revenue, customer counts or interview results from it. Update the public experiment log after verifying current evidence, and validate the problem with real users under appropriate privacy controls.
